    Miss Popularity voting · Built with Jedrick Franconas for Philippine Australian Sports & Culture, Inc.

    A custom voting website for the Miss Popularity award at the 2026 Miss Philippines-Australia & Charity Queen Australia pageant. Voters confirm their email with a one-time code before their vote counts, and Cloudflare Turnstile blocks bots. Organisers get an admin page with a results PDF and a per-voter export.

    My part: I built the /vote page and the vote flow, and updated the admin page to match. I worked through the client's revision rounds: all 9 candidates on one responsive grid, their branded header, background, and fonts, and a candidate card layout that holds up on phones. I also added the admin export of per-voter data as an .xlsx file using a server-side Supabase route, and flagged the privacy trade-off of tying voters to their votes so the client could sign off before using it.

    Outside the code, I worked with the client from August to October: turning their change requests into updates, editing candidate data in Supabase, and troubleshooting with them through the voting week.
